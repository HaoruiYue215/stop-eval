# 宪法誊录：担任总统的最低年龄

**结论：担任美国总统必须年满 35 岁。**

**依据文件：input/article-ii.md**（Article II, Section 1）

原文："neither shall any Person be eligible to that Office who shall not have attained to the Age of thirty five Years, and been fourteen Years a Resident within the United States."（凡未年满三十五岁、且在美国居住满十四年者，不得当选总统。）
