# 结论

叛国罪定罪需要**两名证人**就**同一明显行为**作证。

依据文件：`input/article-iii.md`（Article III, Section 3）。宪法原文为：“No Person shall be convicted of Treason unless on the Testimony of **two Witnesses** to the same overt Act, or on Confession in open Court.”

注：所给 `article-iii.md` 第 20 行在 “Testimony of” 后漏掉了 “two Witnesses”；上述引文已根据该文件所列的[美国国家档案馆宪法誊录](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript)核对。
