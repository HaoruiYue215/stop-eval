需要 **两名证人（two Witnesses）** 对同一明显行为（the same overt Act）作证。

依据文件：`input/article-iii.md`（Article III, Section 3）。该文件的关键句缺漏了“two Witnesses”；按其文首标注的[美国国家档案馆官方誊录](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript)核对，完整原文为：“No Person shall be convicted of Treason unless on the Testimony of two Witnesses to the same overt Act, or on Confession in open Court.”
