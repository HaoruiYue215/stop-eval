# 参议员任期

**结论：参议员的任期是六年（six Years）。**

## 依据

依据文件：`input/article-i.md`（Article I, Section 3）。

其中规定参议院按三组轮换：

> "they shall be divided as equally as may be into three Classes. The Seats of the Senators of the first Class shall be vacated at the Expiration of the second Year, of the second Class at the Expiration of the fourth Year, and of the third Class at the Expiration of the sixth Year, so that one third may be chosen every second Year"

第一组两年期满、第二组四年期满、第三组六年期满，且每两年改选三分之一——由此可知每位参议员任期为 **六年**。

## 需要注意的文本问题

该誊录在直接陈述任期处有缺漏。Section 3 首句写作：

> "chosen by the Legislature thereof, for ; and each Senator shall have one Vote."

"for" 之后缺少 "six Years" 一词（原文应为 "for six Years"）。因此六年这一结论是从同节紧随其后的分组轮换条款推出的，而非直接读到。article-iii.md 与此问题无关。
