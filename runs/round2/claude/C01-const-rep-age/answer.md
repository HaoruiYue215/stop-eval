# 结论

担任众议员必须年满 **二十五岁**。

## 依据

- 文件：`input/article-i.md`（宪法第一条第二款）
- 原文："No Person shall be a Representative who shall not have attained to the Age of twenty five Years, and been seven Years a Citizen of the United States, and who shall not, when elected, be an Inhabitant of that State in which he shall be chosen."

即：凡未满二十五岁、成为合众国公民不满七年、或当选时不是所选州居民者，不得担任众议员。
